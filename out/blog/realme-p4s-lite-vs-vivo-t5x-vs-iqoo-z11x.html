Realme P4s Lite Price in India

The Realme P4s Lite is positioned below the Vivo T5x and iQOO Z11x in terms of starting price. It is designed for buyers who want a large battery and a smooth 120Hz display without spending close to ₹20,000.

Its lower price also comes with some compromises, including a 4G-only connection, HD+ display resolution, a 13MP rear camera and slower 15W charging.

Vivo T5x Price in India

The Vivo T5x 5G starts at around ₹18,999 at launch for the base configuration. The phone is positioned as an all-rounder with a large battery, 5G connectivity, FHD+ 120Hz display and Dimensity 7400-Turbo processor.

The final selling price may vary depending on the RAM and storage configuration, offers and retailer discounts.

iQOO Z11x Price in India

The iQOO Z11x 5G starts at around ₹18,999 for its base configuration. Higher RAM and storage variants can cost more.

iQOO positions the Z11x toward users who want strong performance, gaming capability, long battery life and fast charging.

Which Phone Is Better: Realme P4s Lite vs Vivo T5x vs iQOO Z11x?

The answer depends on what you expect from your smartphone.

Best Budget Phone: Realme P4s Lite

If your budget is limited and you mainly need a phone for WhatsApp, YouTube, Instagram, calling, browsing and basic gaming, the Realme P4s Lite can make sense.

Its 7,000mAh battery and 120Hz display are its biggest advantages. However, the lack of 5G and HD+ display should be considered before purchasing.

Best All-Rounder: Vivo T5x

The Vivo T5x 5G is a better all-rounder for users who want a combination of performance, camera, battery, charging and 5G.

The Dimensity 7400-Turbo processor, 50MP Sony camera, 32MP selfie camera, 7,200mAh battery and 44W charging make it a major upgrade over the Realme P4s Lite.

Best for Gaming: iQOO Z11x

If your priority is gaming and performance, the iQOO Z11x is the strongest choice among these three smartphones.

It combines the Dimensity 7400-Turbo chipset with a 120Hz FHD+ display, 7,200mAh battery, 44W charging and up to 8GB RAM.

Final Verdict

The Realme P4s Lite vs Vivo T5x vs iQOO Z11x comparison shows that each phone is designed for a different type of buyer.

The Realme P4s Lite is the best choice if your main priorities are affordability, large battery capacity and a smooth 120Hz display.

The Vivo T5x 5G is the better balanced option if you want 5G, better cameras, stronger performance, a sharper FHD+ display, 7,200mAh battery and faster 44W charging.

The iQOO Z11x 5G is the better option for performance and gaming-focused users. Its Dimensity 7400-Turbo chipset, 120Hz FHD+ display, 7,200mAh battery and 44W charging make it a strong choice for demanding users.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better, Realme P4s Lite or Vivo T5x?

The Vivo T5x is the better overall phone because it offers a more powerful processor, 5G, FHD+ display, better cameras, a larger battery and faster charging. Realme P4s Lite is better if you have a tighter budget.

Which is better, Vivo T5x or iQOO Z11x?

Both phones offer very similar core hardware, including the Dimensity 7400-Turbo processor, 7,200mAh battery, 44W charging and 50MP Sony primary camera. The Vivo T5x is a strong all-rounder, while the iQOO Z11x is more attractive to performance and gaming-focused buyers.

Which phone has the best battery?

Vivo T5x and iQOO Z11x have the largest battery at 7,200mAh, while Realme P4s Lite has a 7,000mAh battery.

Which phone has the fastest charging?

Vivo T5x and iQOO Z11x support 44W charging, while Realme P4s Lite supports 15W charging.

Does Realme P4s Lite support 5G?

No. Realme P4s Lite is a 4G smartphone. Vivo T5x and iQOO Z11x support 5G connectivity.

Which phone is best for gaming?

The iQOO Z11x is the preferred option for gaming among these three because of its Dimensity 7400-Turbo processor, 120Hz FHD+ display, large 7,200mAh battery and performance-focused positioning.

Which phone has the best camera?

Vivo T5x and iQOO Z11x have significantly stronger camera hardware than the Realme P4s Lite. Both feature a 50MP Sony IMX852 primary camera and 32MP front camera.

Which phone is best under ₹20,000?

If you want the lowest price, Realme P4s Lite is worth considering. If your budget is closer to ₹19,000 and you want 5G, better performance, better cameras and faster charging, Vivo T5x and iQOO Z11x are stronger choices.

Conclusion

Overall, the Realme P4s Lite is a budget-focused smartphone, the Vivo T5x is a balanced all-rounder, and the iQOO Z11x is the more performance-focused option.

For most users who can spend around ₹19,000, the Vivo T5x offers an attractive balance of camera, battery, performance, display and 5G. For gamers and performance enthusiasts, the iQOO Z11x is the better choice. If keeping the purchase price as low as possible is the priority, the Realme P4s Lite remains the budget option.
